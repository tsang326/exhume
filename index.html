Dig up lost files.

Exhume opens 1980s DOS floppy images in your browser and brings deleted files back, as far as the disk allows.

or try a demo disk Opens .img .ima .vfd .flp .dsk, raw floppy images up to 2.88 MB. Drop one anywhere on the page.

Your files stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

No disk? Open one of ours.

Three floppies written for the demo. Pick one and the graveyard below is read live, in this page.

The three demo disks are generated by our own script, scripts/make-demo-disks.ts. They are not real archive disks.
